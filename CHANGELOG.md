# What's new in Vendo

Versions start at 0 while Vendo is a demo. 1.0.0 will be the public launch.

## 0.22.0 · Great venues in your area

_2026-11-17_

- A new row on the home page shows top-rated venues near you. Choose a town, or use your location.
- The town you pick is remembered on your device. If you've already let Vendo use your location, it's used without asking again.
- Until you choose, it shows the top-rated venues on Vendo.

## 0.21.0 · Sliding event and filter menus

_2026-11-17_

- On the home page, events and filters now sit behind two buttons that slide open sideways.
- Picking an event closes the menu and shows your choice on the button. The Filters button shows how many filters are on.

## 0.20.0 · A simpler home page

_2026-11-16_

- A shorter introduction and a simpler search: where, date and guests.
- Feature, price and distance filters now sit behind More filters, and open by themselves when one is in use.
- The budget planner folds away until you open it, and the questions below it are cut to the five people ask most.

## 0.19.2 · Venue types in capitals

_2026-11-16_

- The list of venue types above the main heading now starts each word with a capital: Pubs · Bars · Nightclubs · Village Halls · Clubs · Leisure Centres.

## 0.19.1 · Tidier header on phones

_2026-11-16_

- On phones, the Book a venue, For venues and Admin tabs no longer squash onto two lines. When they can't fit beside the logo they get their own row.

## 0.19.0 · A cleaner look

_2026-11-16_

- A new typeface, Inter Tight, across the whole site.
- Crisper colours, smaller corners, quieter shadows and plain-English labels instead of small capitals.
- Proper icons in place of text symbols.

## 0.18.1 · Sign in on iPhone

_2026-11-16_

- Create an account and sign in inside the iPhone app, with the 6-digit code from the email.
- Sign-in emails now say "code" everywhere, since every email includes one.

## 0.18.0 · Add and verify venues

_2026-11-16_

- Venue owners can add their own venue: rooms and prices, extras, photos, house rules and cancellation policy. Save a draft and submit it when ready.
- To get verified, owners upload photo ID, their premises licence and insurance certificate. Only the Vendo team can open these documents.
- In the Admin tab, review each venue, tick off the three checks, then approve it or send it back with a note.
- The Verified venue badge now shows only on venues Vendo has checked. New venues show their own photos, and "New" until they have reviews.

## 0.17.0 · Admin dashboard

_2026-11-16_

- A private Admin tab for the Vendo team: takings, profit, bookings, customers and venues, with monthly charts.
- Approve new venues before they go live, and keep a list of running costs so profit is worked out for you.
- Only people on the admin list can see it. The figures come from the database, which refuses everyone else.

## 0.16.0 · Create an account

_2026-11-16_

- A proper sign-up page: your name and email, whether you're planning events or run a venue, and your venue's details if you do.
- A separate sign-in page that tells you if there's no account for your email.
- After signing in, venue owners go to their dashboard, guests to their account, and anyone mid-booking straight back to it.

## 0.15.1 · Our own address

_2026-11-16_

- Vendo now lives at vendohire.com, with a secure connection. The old vendohire.netlify.app address takes you there automatically.

## 0.15.0 · Getting there by public transport

_2026-11-16_

- Each venue page shows the nearest stations, tram stops and bus stops, with walking times and step-free details.
- See the bus numbers that stop nearby, a map of the stops, and a link to plan your journey.

## 0.14.1 · Clearer sign-in limits

_2026-11-16_

- If too many sign-in emails have been sent, the message now says whether to wait seconds or an hour.

## 0.14.0 · Live database and sign-in

_2026-11-16_

- Venues, rooms, extras and reviews now load from the Vendo database, with the example venues as a backup if it can't be reached.
- Calendars show real booked dates from the database.
- Sign in with an emailed link: no password needed. Your account shows your bookings and details.
- Booking requests and saved venues are stored in your account once you're signed in.

## 0.13.1 · Cleaner page addresses

_2026-11-15_

- Page addresses like /about no longer redirect to /about/, so search engines see one address for each page.

## 0.13.0 · Search engines

_2026-11-15_

- Every page now has its own web address, like /about and /venues/the-plough-and-harrow, with its own title and description for Google and for link previews.
- A sitemap and robots file help search engines find the right pages. Venue pages stay out of search results until the venues are real.
- Pages load faster: the map only loads when you open it.
- Old links with a # still work and move to the new address.

## 0.12.0 · iPhone app

_2026-11-15_

- Vendo is now an iPhone app as well as a website, built from the same code so every update reaches both.
- In the app: a Vendo icon and launch screen, the iPhone share sheet for venues, a light tap feel when you save a venue or book, and calendar files and statements saved through the share sheet.

## 0.11.0 · Booking page

_2026-11-15_

- A full booking page: event details, your details, payment and the venue's policies, with the price summary always in view.
- Choose to pay a deposit or in full. Request-to-book venues take nothing until they accept.
- A confirmation page with what happens next, a calendar file and a message button.
- New bookings appear in your account with an event checklist, and on the venue's calendar.

## 0.10.0 · Version numbers

_2026-11-15_

- Every release now has a version number, shown in the footer.
- A What's new page lists every release.

## 0.9.0 · Planning tools, venue tabs and trust

- Verified, fast-reply and top host badges, Booking Protection and guest photos on reviews.
- Installable app, structured data for search engines and accessibility fixes.
